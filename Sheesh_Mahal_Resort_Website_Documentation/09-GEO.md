# GEO – Generative Engine Optimisation

GEO aims to make accurate business information easier for generative search systems to discover and cite. No checklist guarantees inclusion or ranking.

## Source of truth
Make the official site the clearest source for venue name, address, phone, services and social profiles. Keep Google Business Profile and official social details consistent.

## Verifiable content
Use specific factual statements, original approved photography, directions, useful FAQs, current policies and structured data that matches visible content.

## Local authority
Maintain a complete Google Business Profile, accurate reputable listings, genuine customer reviews and legitimate local/event mentions. Do not buy spam links or fabricate awards/reviews.

## Technical discoverability
Allow legitimate crawlers to access public pages, keep sitemap current, use descriptive headings and internal links, and avoid hiding key content behind inaccessible scripts.

## Avoid
No mass-generated low-value pages, hidden keyword text, fabricated citations, or promises of placement in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini or other answer engines.

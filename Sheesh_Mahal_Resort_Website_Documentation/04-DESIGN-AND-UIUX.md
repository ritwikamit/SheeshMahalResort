# Design System and UI/UX Specification

## Creative direction
Elegant, celebratory and photo-led. Use real venue imagery rather than stock palace imagery or visual claims that do not match the property.

## Proposed palette
- Deep charcoal `#151313`
- Warm ivory `#F7F2EA`
- Champagne gold `#B99A62`
- Muted gold `#8C7042`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Body text `#302B27`
Gold should be an accent, not the colour for long body text. Check contrast.

## Typography
Use an elegant serif for headings (e.g. Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display) and a clean sans serif for body/UI (e.g. Inter or Manrope). Decorative script only for small accents. No typewriter fonts.

## Imagery
Use owner-approved exterior/entrance photos, hall/stage/seating, event decoration, dining setup only if relevant, access/parking if helpful. Obtain permission for identifiable customers and event photography.

## Motion
Subtle transitions only; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no autoplay sound. Hero video should have poster image, compression and static fallback.

## Mobile UX
Prominent but unobtrusive Call/WhatsApp action; large tap targets; short enquiry form; date-not-finalised option; address/directions visible; no blocking popup.

## Components
Header, hero, event cards, verified facility list, accessible gallery/lightbox, semantic FAQ accordion, enquiry form with accessible errors, location/map directions, footer with business details and privacy/social links.

## Content style
Warm, clear and specific. Avoid “best in Patna” or other unsupported superlatives. Do not show prices, capacity, facilities or policies until approved.

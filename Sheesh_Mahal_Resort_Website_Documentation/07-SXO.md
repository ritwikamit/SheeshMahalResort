# SXO – Search Experience Optimisation

SXO connects search visibility with useful user experience and conversion.

## Visitors should quickly understand
- What the venue is.
- Where it is.
- What events it can host.
- What it looks like.
- How to check a date or contact the team.

## Reduce friction
Place Call, WhatsApp and Check Date actions near the top. Show address/directions. Keep the form short. Explain that an enquiry is not a confirmed booking. Do not require an account. Provide phone/WhatsApp fallback if the form fails.

## Trust
Use authentic photos, consistent contact details, verified facilities/policies and real testimonials only with permission. Add a privacy notice near the form. Avoid fake scarcity, fabricated ratings and unsupported superlatives.

## Accessibility and mobile
Use large tap targets, keyboard-accessible navigation/gallery, visible focus, sufficient contrast, proper labels/errors, image alt text, semantic headings and reduced-motion support. Test on mid-range Android devices and mobile networks.

## Performance targets
Aim for Core Web Vitals: LCP <= 2.5s, INP <= 200ms and CLS <= 0.1. These are targets, not guaranteed outcomes. Measure after launch.

## Conversion tracking
Track calls, WhatsApp clicks, form submissions and directions. Never include phone numbers, names or message contents in analytics events.

# AEO – Answer Engine Optimisation

## Answer-ready business facts
Once verified, publish a consistent block with official name, address/map pin, phone/WhatsApp, hours, event categories, spaces/capacity, catering/decor policies, parking/accessibility and enquiry process.

## FAQ topics
- Where is Sheesh Mahal Resort located in Patna?
- How do I enquire about a wedding date?
- Which events can be hosted?
- How do I get directions?
- How do I ask about packages?
- Is my preferred date available?

Give short direct answers that match visible page content. Remove or update stale facts promptly.

## Structured data
Use JSON-LD with verified fields only. Markup must match visible content. Structured data improves machine readability but does not guarantee rich results or answer-engine inclusion.

## Content quality
Use clear headings, short paragraphs and natural landmark context. Avoid keyword stuffing, repetitive FAQs and fabricated information.

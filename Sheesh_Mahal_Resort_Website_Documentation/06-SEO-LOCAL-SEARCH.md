# SEO and Local Search Optimisation

## Search-intent hypotheses
- wedding venue in Patna
- wedding resort in Patna
- banquet hall near St. Xavier's College Patna
- reception venue in Patna
- engagement venue in Patna
- birthday party venue in Patna
- wedding venue Ashiana Digha Road
- Sheesh Mahal Resort contact number / directions
These are ideas to validate in Search Console and keyword research, not claims of search volume.

## Page architecture
Home; Weddings & Receptions; Pre-Wedding Functions; Family Events; Gallery; Contact. Build pages only where unique useful content exists; avoid doorway pages that merely change location keywords.

## On-page
Unique title/description, one clear H1, descriptive H2s, canonical URL, Open Graph, internal links, image alt text, responsive compressed images and accurate NAP.

## Technical SEO
XML sitemap, appropriate robots.txt, HTTPS, canonical-domain redirect, no indexing preview/admin/API routes, proper 404/410 responses, no mixed content, Search Console monitoring.

## Local SEO
Claim and verify the correct Google Business Profile. Confirm map pin, name, address, phone, hours, category and services. Add current original venue photos. Ask genuine customers for honest reviews without incentives. Respond professionally and do not reveal private customer details. Keep official website and listings consistent.

## Structured data
Use an appropriate LocalBusiness subtype only when details are verified. Use address, telephone, URL, geo, hours, images and sameAs only when accurate. Do not invent ratings or mark up invisible/fabricated reviews.

## Measurement
Monitor Search Console impressions, queries, CTR, index coverage and issues. Track call/WhatsApp/form/directions conversions without sending personal form content to analytics.
